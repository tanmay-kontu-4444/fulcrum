Scouting metrics that read the game in context.

Fulcrum evaluates player performance relative to role, match circumstance, team strength, and opposition quality. It surfaces talent that raw counting stats miss — undervalued creators on mid-table sides, destroyers whose work never reaches the scoresheet, keepers who quietly hold high lines. Built as a scouting layer, not a replacement for traditional ratings.

04 · Methodology

What the score measures

The context-adjusted score answers one question: given this player’s role, the match state, the team around them, and the opposition they faced, are they performing above expectation?

01 / ACTION VALUE

Every action carries a possession-value

Each on-ball and off-ball action is priced by how much it changed the probability of a goal in the following possession sequence. A recovery under high press is worth more than an unpressured back-pass; a chance-creating cutback is worth more than an aimless cross.

AV(a) = P(goal | state after a) − P(goal | state before a)
02 / ROLE EXPECTATION

Baseline set by role, not position

Position labels (CB, ST) are too coarse. Fulcrum classifies each player into one of thirty-two fine-grained roles — ball-playing CB, destroyer DM, half-space creator — and models the expected AV output for that role given league and minutes played.

Δrole = AVactual − E[AV | role, minutes]
03 / CONTEXT ADJUSTMENT

Match state, team strength, opposition

A goal scored while chasing at 0-2 against a top-four side is not the same event as a goal scored leading at home against a relegation-side. A multiplier stack of match state, team-strength gap, and opponent quality tunes each action’s contribution.

Contrib(a) = AV(a) · Mstate · Mgap · Mopp
04 / WHY IT MATTERS

Built for scouting decisions

Traditional ratings reward volume on strong teams. The context-adjusted score is designed to surface the destroyer keeping a bottom-six side afloat, the young creator generating chances against low blocks, the keeper whose distribution enables a possession identity. It is a scouting lens, not a rating replacement.

Score = 5 · σ(Σ Contrib per 90 · sample_conf) + 3
